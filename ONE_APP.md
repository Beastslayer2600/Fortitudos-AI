# One app. One startup.

Double-click **Start Fortitudo Desk.bat**.

Paste in **Learn & ingest** → Teach all three branches.
Sales, design, psychology, misc — one note feeds Advisor, Studio and Craft (`learn:all:`).
Product PDFs stay on the product shelf. Client files stay on the client.

Advisor never teaches itself: it answers from filed pages, and model-written
material (Craft lessons, Sight extracts, AI drafts) is kept out of its corpus.
