Occupational Disability — Waiting period
The waiting period options are 7 days, 14 days, one month, three months or
six months. The benefit begins to accrue at the end of the chosen waiting
period and is paid monthly in arrears.

Temporary Disability
A temporary disability claim requires that the life insured is unable to
perform the material duties of their own occupation. The maximum benefit is
75% of pre-disability income.
