Severe Illness Benefit — Cancer
A claim for cancer is assessed against the severity levels in this section.
Level A pays 100% of the benefit amount. Level B pays 60%. Level C pays 30%.
Level D pays 15%. Carcinoma in situ of the breast is a Level C event.
There is a survival period of 7 days from the date of diagnosis.
%%PAGE 13
Severe Illness Benefit — Waiting periods
A waiting period of 6 months applies from the commencement date for all
severe illness events other than accidental injury. No waiting period applies
where the event is the direct result of an accident.
