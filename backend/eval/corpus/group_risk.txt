Group Risk — Waiting periods and deferred periods
A deferred period of 3 months applies before the group income benefit begins.
The scheme waiting period for new entrants is 3 months from date of joining.
This is a group scheme and is not the individual Lifestyle Protector benefit.
%%PAGE 8
Group Risk — Severe illness
The group severe illness benefit pays a lump sum on a Level A event.
Severity levels and survival periods follow the scheme rules, not the
individual policy wording.
