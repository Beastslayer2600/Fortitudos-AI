Severe Illness Benefit — Cancer
A claim for cancer is assessed against the severity levels in this section.
Level A pays 100% of the benefit amount. Level B pays 50%. Level C pays 25%.
Level D pays 10%. Carcinoma in situ of the breast is a Level D event.
There is a survival period of 14 days from the date of diagnosis.
%%PAGE 13
Severe Illness Benefit — Waiting periods
A waiting period of 3 months applies from the commencement date for all
severe illness events other than accidental injury. No waiting period applies
where the event is the direct result of an accident.
%%PAGE 27
Hearing Loss
Total and irreversible loss of hearing in both ears is a Level A event where
the loss exceeds 90 decibels across all frequencies in the better ear, tested
by pure tone audiometry. Loss between 70 and 90 decibels is a Level C event.
%%PAGE 41
Exclusions
No benefit is payable where the claim arises from self-inflicted injury within
24 months of the commencement date, participation in active military service,
or a condition that existed before the commencement date and was not disclosed.
