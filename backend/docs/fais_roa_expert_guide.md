# FAIS & Record of Advice — Expert Practice Guide (South Africa)

**Purpose:** Operating standard for Fortitudo AI drafts and client files.  
**Role of the AI:** Evidence and drafting assistant for a licensed adviser. Not automated advice. Not an FSP.

## Legal backbone (practical reading)

Under the FAIS General Code of Conduct, a provider (including a representative) must:

1. Take reasonable steps to obtain information on the client’s financial situation, experience and objectives.
2. Conduct an analysis based on that information.
3. Identify a suitable financial product given needs and risk profile.
4. Where a product replaces an existing one, disclose material implications and costs of replacement.
5. **Maintain a record of the advice** reflecting the **basis** on which advice was given.

Minimum retention: **five years** (FAIS / General Code practice). Inability to produce an ROA has led to Ombud settlements (e.g. missing ROA cited in jewellery claim disputes).

The FSCA’s practical standard is reconstructability: a third party (auditor, investigator, Tribunal) should be able to see **why this product was recommended for this client at this time**.

## What “basis of advice” must allow reconstruction of

1. Client needs analysis at the point of advice (circumstances, objectives, risk).
2. Product information considered.
3. Alternatives assessed and reasons rejected (or explicitly “only one suitable class considered”).
4. Recommendation and **client-specific** rationale (not a generic brochure dump).
5. Material risks and limitations (including waiting periods, survival periods, definitions, exclusions where relevant).
6. Fees and charges disclosed.
7. Replacement comparison if applicable.
8. Client acknowledgment / signature or electronic confirmation where required by process.

Common FSCA finding: templated ROAs with little client-specific analysis.

## Fortitudo ROA working draft structure (internal)

Use this section order in AI “ROA structure” drafts. Every factual claim about a product needs `[EVIDENCE: document, page]` or `[MISSING]`.

1. **INTERNAL DRAFT — ADVISER REVIEW REQUIRED**
2. **Engagement / service scope** — what was requested; limits of the engagement.
3. **Client circumstances & needs** — only from filed FNA / notes / documents.
4. **Objectives & risk posture** — as documented; flag gaps.
5. **Information relied on** — list filed documents (FICA, RPQ, quotes, existing policies).
6. **Products / options considered** — name classes and any quotes on file.
7. **Recommendation & rationale** — map features to stated needs; no invented figures.
8. **Material risks, limitations, definitions** — waiting/survival periods, severity tiers, exclusions → evidence tags.
9. **Costs, fees, charges** — from quotes or `[MISSING]`.
10. **Replacement analysis** — if replacing; else “N/A — no replacement indicated in file”.
11. **Outstanding information / FICA** — checklist.
12. **Adviser confirmation checklist** — human sign-off items before client presentation.

## FICA / file hygiene (operational)

- Identity and verification documents filed under client vault (offline).
- Source of funds / ongoing monitoring per your FSP’s RMCP — AI does not replace compliance officer process.
- POPIA: purpose limitation; client data outside OneDrive where policy requires (FortitudoData path).

## How to use Fortitudo AI without crossing the advice line

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Retrieve cited product wording | Tell the end client “buy this” |
| Draft internal ROA shells | Publish AI text as final advice without review |
| Evidence packs for technical transparency | Invent benefit % or waiting periods |
| Meeting-prep document gaps | Claim the system is licensed advice |

## Evidence Pack (client-facing supplement)

Not a letter of advice. A technical annex that explains mechanics (e.g. 14-day survival on a specific benefit, severity percentages) with document/page citations the adviser has verified. Builds trust through precision; still under adviser control.

## Ombud / audit readiness test

Before finalising any file, ask: *If the Ombud asked tomorrow for the ROA on this recommendation, could we produce individualised needs, products considered, rationale, risks and fees in one coherent record?* If not, the file is not ready.
