# Craft design doctrine

A Fortitudo Craft page exists so a stranger in that suburb can call in one tap.
It is not a brand film, not a blog, not a Wix theme.

## The job of the page

Someone already has intent: geyser, burst pipe, bald tyre, Saturday haircut.
They searched or they were handed a flyer. The page must:
1. Confirm they have the right shop and the right suburb.
2. Put Call and WhatsApp in the thumb zone.
3. Repeat NAP (name, address, phone) so Maps and Google can trust it.
4. Stop. Do not add a third CTA.

Primary CTA is always Call. Secondary is WhatsApp. Email is last, if it exists.

## Marketing truth for this SKU

- Google Business Profile often outranks the site for "plumber near me". The site still matters when GBP is thin, when they share a link, and when a flyer QR opens on a phone.
- Headline formula: job + suburb. Bad: "Quality you can trust". Good: "Burst pipe in Kempton Park — call Joe".
- Social proof only if it is in the brief (a named review, a year on the door). Never invent stars.
- Price of *their* labour stays off the page unless they gave it. Our R5,500 is for the flyer, not their site.
- Emergency trades (plumb, electrical, geyser, locksmith): first screen is panic-calm. Appointment trades (salon, cafe): first screen is place + hours.

## Design decisions the model must make

- Audience: one person, one job, one suburb.
- Mood: industrial (workshop/trade), warm (bakery/cafe), cool (salon/practice), lush (plants/lodge).
- When you are writing a spec, type and colour belong to the renderer — do not
  describe hex codes or fonts. When you are authoring the document yourself
  (see craft_html_doctrine.md), they are yours to choose.
- Photos: only theirs. If none, say so in missing. No stock kitchens.
- Hours: print only if seen on the door or said by the owner.
- Language: South African English. Short. Grade 6.

## What a page must never do

Invent 24/7, awards, "best in Gauteng", fake reviews, client counts, or a second location.
Add sliders, autoplay video, popups, or a blog.
Hide the phone in a form.
Speak like a Sandton agency.

## Flyer

One shop name. One line that matches the headline job. QR to the hosted mock.
Your number is on the paper. The page does the rest.
