## What the word means

Fortitudo is Latin for strength, courage, endurance. The practice uses it as discipline that holds under pressure — not as a gym slogan and not as a property developer.

## What this house actually is

One person, several doors, one temperament.

1. **Fortitudo Studios / Wealth** — fortitudostudios.site
   Gert Fourie, representative of Liberty Group Limited, FSP 2409, Pretoria.
   Public line: “You work hard for your money. Let's structure it to endure.”
   Audience: high-income professionals who earn well and still feel quiet anxiety.
   Promise: structure over speculation, discipline over emotion, clarity not complexity.
   Process: strategy session → financial analysis → structured plan → ongoing review.
   Health check scores seven pressure points: reserve, income continuation, dependants, retirement rate, debt structure, education funding, estate readiness.
   Legal posture: general information only; personalised advice only after a needs analysis.

2. **Fortitudo Agency** — fortitudo.agency
   “We build it. You own it.” Fixed price. Senior people. AI for repeat steps; a person checks every change. Seven visible stages. Client owns files the day it goes live.

3. **Fortitudo Craft** — shop factory in the desk
   Jobs → Audit → 3D noun test → Page → Send. One page that looks like the trade. One letter, not a blast.

4. **Fortitudo Drama / Studio** — fortitudostudios-drama.vercel.app
   Online eisteddfod coaching. Adjudicator-led.
   “Nerves are not the enemy. An unprepared entrance is.”
   “The mark sheet is not a mystery when the person teaching you writes them.”

5. **Fortitudo Desk** — this app. One launcher. Local Ollama. FAIS: the desk drafts; you remain the representative.

## What the brand is becoming

Not five startups. One house. Structure you can look at: forty years as stacked decisions, a shop as one screen, a performance as one entrance, an app as one window.

## Voice rules

Short. Concrete. Cite SOURCE and PAGE. Shame is not a method. Dark editorial look. AI drafts; a person signs.
