## Privacy-first differentiator
Unlike tools that send client files to cloud models, this practice runs a **local-only intelligence workspace**.
- Sensitive documents stay on encrypted local storage.
- No third-party training on your data.
- Product answers are retrieved from indexed technical guides with page citations.

## Method bullets
- Index the latest product technical guides (page-level, including benefit tables).
- Ground recommendations in cited contract wording (waiting periods, severity definitions, exclusions).
- Separate client files from product research; both remain offline.
- Adviser judgement owns the final advice; the system is an evidence engine.

## FAQ seeds
- **What happens after I contact you?** A short call to confirm fit, then a clear plan of what information is needed. No pressure, no newsletter by default.
- **How is my information handled?** Used only to respond and to deliver advice. Client files are stored locally under controlled access — not uploaded to public AI services by this workspace.
- **Do you invent product figures?** No. Benefit percentages, waiting periods and definitions are taken from provider technical guides and cited by document and page where used.
- **Is this a live website?** Mockups generated inside Fortitudo AI are internal drafts until you publish them on your own host after review.
